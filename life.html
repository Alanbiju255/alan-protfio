Broken, Rebuilt & Resilient

The untold journey behind the code — navigating childhood harassment, bitter betrayal, and lonely nights, to discovering programming, building 3 teams with 15 people, and refusing to ever surrender.

By Alan Biju
5 Min Read
A Story of Survival & Strength

My life hasn’t always been easy. Behind the founder titles, the 53+ delivered software projects, and the national records, there is a quieter, raw truth that shaped who I am today. It is a story not forged in comfort, but sculpted through pain, isolation, and an unrelenting refusal to stay down.

Chapter 01 · The Early Years (Grades 4 through 10)

The Burden of Silence & Broken Trust

From a very young age, I faced profound mental and physical struggles. When I was just in 4th grade, I experienced physical harassment. For a young child, the world is supposed to be safe. You expect the adults around you to protect you. But sadly, instead of supporting me, some of the people around me—including teachers who held my trust—chose to support the wrong person.

The injustice cut deeper than any physical blow. That same painful pattern continued relentlessly through 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, and even into 10th grade. Year after year, I lived with the agonizing feeling that the truth didn’t matter to the people who were supposed to safeguard me.

“

"I was completely broken."

When innocence is met with betrayal, survival becomes the only daily assignment.

Chapter 02 · The Tuition Years (Ages 15 – 17)

Shaken at the Crossroads

When I was around 15 to 17 years old, I joined a tuition class, hoping with all my heart that things would finally be different. I thought a new environment with new faces would offer a clean slate and a breath of fresh air.

Instead, history cruelly repeated itself. There too, I experienced physical and mental harassment from a teacher. The sense of helplessness was suffocating. Once again, I felt like I was falling completely apart, watching the ground crumble beneath my feet.

Chapter 03 · The Turning Point (10th Grade)

The Sanctuary of the Screen: Discovering Programming

Then, in 10th grade, a quiet transformation began. I discovered computer programming.

I started spending more and more time in front of computer monitors. In a world full of arbitrary judgments, cruel gossip, and broken promises, the machine offered something radical: purity and fairness.

sanctuary.py — Alan's First Real Refuge
# The one place where truth was absolute
class Sanctuary:
  def __init__(self):
    self.judgment = None
    self.blame = None
    self.voice = "Uncensored & Free"

  def build(self, dreams):
    return dreams.compile_into_reality()

The one thing I felt could never judge me, blame me, or hurt me was a computer. A compiler didn't care about the rumors. An algorithm didn't bully me. If my syntax was correct, the machine obeyed with unwavering honesty. It became my personal sanctuary—a sacred space where I could learn, create, and express myself without fear.

Chapter 04 · The Solitary Years (+2 High School)

Sitting Alone in the Shadows

During my +2 years, I often sat alone. I had very few friends, but somehow, day after day, I learned to live with the silence.

All those past experiences had carved deep, bleeding wounds into my mind. There were heavy, dark nights when I stared at the ceiling and truly believed that my entire existence was a waste—that maybe I was fundamentally unsuited for this world.

“

"But I didn’t give up."

Deep within the ashes, an unspoken stubbornness kept burning.

Chapter 05 · College Battles & Early Exploitation

Tested by the Working World

When I stepped into college, I didn't wait around for permission. I immediately began doing freelance and part-time work, crafting web development projects and tackling every piece of engineering technology I could get my hands on. I held big dreams of becoming successful, of proving that my mind had value.

Eventually, I joined an external team. But unfortunately, they chose to exploit my hunger and skill. I poured hours into complex systems, only to receive little or no payment. I was treated poorly, spoken down to, and even blamed for catastrophic issues I had no part in creating. I was treated like a toy—used when convenient, and tossed aside without a second thought.

Once again, I found myself pushed down to the dirt.

Chapter 06 · The Unshakable Foundation

The Unconditional Love of My Parents

Every time I was broken before, I had felt utterly alone. But this time, I had something different—people who genuinely believed in me.

My Mother & Father

The Unsung Heroes of My Life

When everyone else doubted, exploited, or turned away, my mother and father stood unwavering beside me. They sheltered me through the emotional storms, listened when I had no words, and believed in my capabilities when the world was blind to them. Their support gave me the bedrock strength to stand up, dust myself off, and build something purely my own.

Chapter 07 · Where I Stand Today

3 Teams, 15 People & A Tribe of True Believers

Today, I am proud to say that I run three teams with around 15 talented, dedicated people working right beside me across Aaronbyte Studios and AlanPod Space.

I have achieved things that the broken 10th grader in the back row once thought were impossible. I've had the honor to connect with celebrated figures, made genuine friends from completely diverse walks of life—including brave individuals serving in the Indian Army—and encountered so many extraordinary mentors and peers who champion our mission.

And I know, with all my heart, that I did not reach this summit alone.
